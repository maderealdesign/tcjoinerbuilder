Before you begin

Your questions.
Clear answers.

Planning a garden room, weighing up decking or thinking about work inside the house? These are a few useful starting points.

How much does a garden room or decking project cost?

The price depends on the size, materials, access, ground preparation and finish. A garden room’s intended use and requirements for insulation, lighting and power also affect the scope. Share your ideas and budget so we can arrange a site visit and prepare a quotation for the agreed work.

Can you build a garden room and decking together?

Yes. Planning both together lets us consider the entrance, steps, seating, levels and the route back to the house. You can also ask about a pergola or other joinery as part of the outdoor space.

What can I use a garden room for?

A garden office, home gym, hobby studio or space to relax. Tell us how you want to use it and whether that will change through the year, so we can discuss the layout, insulation, ventilation and any services your project needs.

Do you still carry out other joinery and building work?

Yes. Our work includes kitchen fitting, fitted wardrobes, media walls, panelling, internal doors, staircases, extensions and renovations, alongside commercial joinery and site management. Garden rooms and decking are part of that wider experience.

Are you insured, and how long have you been established?

Tom Cutts Joinery & Building was established in 2020 and is backed by Tom’s 18+ years of hands-on and managerial experience. We carry up to £5 million public liability insurance for domestic and commercial work.

Do you manage the project as well as carry out the work?

We offer building and joinery work alongside project management, with experience on both domestic projects and commercial sites. We will discuss the level of coordination your job needs and make the responsibilities clear before work begins.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Colne and cover Pendle, Burnley and wider Lancashire, including Clitheroe, Whalley and Barrow in the Ribble Valley. We also work towards Silsden, Sutton and Cross Hills. Send your postcode and a short description of the work so we can confirm coverage for your project.

What should I send when asking for a quotation?

Your postcode, a few photos, approximate measurements and a short description of your idea are a helpful start. Include your preferred budget and timescale if you have them. You do not need a finished design to start the conversation; quotations are free and without obligation.
