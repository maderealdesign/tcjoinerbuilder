Joinery & building in
Lancashire.

Tom Cutts Joinery & Building is based in Colne and serves Lancashire projects, with a regular focus on Pendle, Burnley and the Ribble Valley. For work beyond the named local areas, send the postcode and scope first. Travel, project size and timing all matter when confirming a wider-area enquiry.

Choose the right starting point for your brief

For a garden room or deck, use the dedicated service guides to compare the specification and information needed for a quote. For commercial joinery or site management, send drawings, the proposed programme and the work package. A county-wide enquiry can involve very different levels of coordination, so be clear about the role you need our team to take.

Confirm coverage and responsibilities before booking

We do not claim a branch in every Lancashire town. Confirm the site postcode, access arrangements and desired timing with the Colne team. For projects involving designers or other contractors, identify the main contact and which permissions, specifications and site arrangements are already in place.

Your project checklist

  • Send the site postcode and an outline of the complete work package.
  • Include drawings, specifications and the proposed programme if available.
  • Identify other contractors and the person coordinating decisions.
