Bespoke joinery, with care in every detail.

Well-planned joinery makes a home easier to use and brings the details together. Tom Cutts Joinery & Building carries out bespoke joinery and carpentry across Colne, Pendle, the Ribble Valley and surrounding areas, from fitted storage and media walls to doors, staircases and the timber work within a larger build.

Discuss your project ↗

Tom’s 18+ years of hands-on and managerial experience spans both domestic properties and commercial sites. Whether you have one room in mind or need joinery throughout a renovation, we take time to understand the space, the finish and how the work fits into your wider plans.

Fitted storage that uses the space you have

Alcoves, awkward corners and the space beneath a staircase can all offer useful storage. Start with what you need to store: hanging clothes, books, everyday essentials or larger items. We can discuss the balance of shelves, cupboards and open space, then consider proportions, access and the finish in relation to the room.

Media walls, panelling and fitted features

A media wall should work with the equipment you own and the way you use the room. TV size, cable routes, shelving and any proposed electric fire need to be considered early. Decorative panelling and fitted features can also bring character to a hallway, bedroom or living space without losing sight of practical details.

Doors, staircases and the finishing work

Internal doors, skirting, architraves and window boards make a noticeable difference to the finished home. We also work on staircases and balustrades. Share photographs of the existing fittings and examples of the look you want, particularly where new joinery needs to sit alongside features you intend to keep.

Joinery as part of a bigger project

For extensions, renovations and commercial projects, joinery needs to be coordinated with the other work. We carry out first-fix work such as timber framing and studwork alongside second-fix finishing. Drawings, a specification and an outline programme help us discuss the scope, sequencing and the level of site support you need.

Useful answers

Before you begin.

Can you make fitted storage for an awkward space?

Yes. Send photographs, approximate dimensions and a description of what you want to store. We can discuss a layout that makes use of the available space and suits the surrounding room.

What is the difference between first- and second-fix joinery?

First-fix joinery generally covers timber work before plastering, such as studwork and door linings. Second-fix covers visible finishing work, including internal doors, skirting, architraves and other fitted details.

Can you build a media wall around my existing TV?

Yes, we can discuss a design around your equipment. Share the TV dimensions and details of any fire, speakers, shelving or lighting you want included so these can be considered together.

Do you take on commercial joinery projects?

Yes. Our work includes joinery for commercial fit-outs, developments and refurbishment projects. Send the drawings or scope, site location and proposed programme so we can discuss the support your project requires.
