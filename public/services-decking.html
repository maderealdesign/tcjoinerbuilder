Decking that makes more of your garden.

Create a place to sit, eat and spend time outdoors. Tom Cutts Joinery & Building installs decking for homes across Colne, Pendle, Clitheroe, Whalley and the surrounding area. Whether you want a defined seating space or a deck outside a new garden room, we help turn the idea into a practical layout.

Discuss your project ↗

We look at how the deck will meet the house, garden and existing levels before discussing the finish. The result starts with a clear plan for the whole space, supported by Tom’s background in joinery, building and project management.

Choose a layout for everyday use

Think about where your table, chairs and other furniture will sit, and leave room to move around them. A deck also needs to work with doors, paths and access to the rest of the garden. We can discuss steps, edges and changes in level so the layout feels considered from every approach.

Compare timber and composite finishes

Timber and composite boards offer different looks and care requirements. We discuss the appearance you want, your budget and the product options suitable for the job. Board colour, direction, edging and visible fixings all contribute to the finished space. Ask us to explain the proposed specification and maintenance expectations before you make your choice.

Look beneath the finished boards

A decking project involves more than choosing the surface. Existing ground, support, drainage, access and any removal work affect the scope. If you are replacing an old deck, photographs of the boards and the structure beneath are helpful. We assess what the new installation needs and make the preparation clear in the quotation.

Plan decking and a garden room together

A deck can extend the usable space outside a garden office, studio or room for relaxing. Planning the two together helps coordinate door positions, levels, seating and the route to your home. For features such as a hot tub or heavy equipment, tell us at the start so the relevant support requirements can be considered in the project design.

Useful answers

Before you begin.

Can you replace existing decking?

Yes. Send photos of the current deck, including any visible damage and the structure beneath where accessible. We will discuss removal, preparation and the specification for the replacement before quoting.

Which is better for my garden: timber or composite?

The best choice depends on the look you prefer, budget, product specification and care requirements. We can explain the options for your project so you can compare them on a clear basis.

Can the design include steps or a pergola?

Yes, these can be discussed as part of the project. Photos of the garden and approximate measurements help us understand the available space, changes in level and the kind of covered or open seating area you want.

What information do you need for a decking quote?

Your postcode, a few photos and approximate dimensions are a useful start. Tell us whether an existing deck needs removing, what you want to put on the deck and whether you have a preferred material or budget.
