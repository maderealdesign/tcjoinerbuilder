Bespoke garden rooms, built around your life.

Give work, exercise or hobbies a space of their own. Tom Cutts Joinery & Building creates bespoke garden rooms for homeowners in Colne, Pendle, the Ribble Valley and surrounding areas. We start with how you want to use the room, then work through the layout, materials and practical details together.

Discuss your project ↗

Your garden room should make sense for both your home and your garden. With 18+ years of hands-on construction and management experience, Tom brings building knowledge and careful joinery to the project, from the structure through to the finishing details.

Start with what the room needs to do

For a home office, think about desk space, daylight, sockets and the route back to the house. A gym needs a layout that suits your equipment; a studio may need more wall space or storage. Tell us what matters most so the footprint, doors and windows support the way you will actually use the room.

Discuss comfort, services and finishes early

We discuss insulation, ventilation, glazing and the internal finish as part of the specification. Power, lighting, heating and internet requirements should also be considered before the work begins. This gives us a clear picture of the associated trades and preparation your project needs, rather than leaving essential details to the end.

Connect the room to your garden

Decking can create a useful place to sit outside the room and help connect the entrance with the rest of the garden. Planning both together lets us consider steps, changes in level and routes around the building. We can also discuss a pergola or bespoke outdoor joinery as part of the wider space.

A quotation based on your actual site

Size is only one part of a garden-room quotation. Access, the existing ground, preparation, materials and services all affect the work involved. Share photographs, approximate dimensions, your postcode and a preferred budget. We can then discuss a site visit and prepare a quotation with a clear scope. Required permissions and professional input should be resolved before construction starts.

Useful answers

Before you begin.

Can a garden room be designed for use throughout the year?

Yes, that can be part of the brief. Tell us when and how you intend to use it so we can discuss insulation, glazing, ventilation and heating requirements. The agreed specification will set out what is included.

Can you build decking at the same time?

Yes. A combined project lets us plan the entrance, outdoor seating, steps and connection to the house together. We can also quote for decking as a separate project.

Do I need a finished design before getting in touch?

No. An idea of the room’s purpose, approximate size and budget is enough to begin. Photos showing the proposed position and access to the garden are particularly useful.

How much will my garden room cost?

We quote for the individual project. The size, specification, ground preparation, access and required services all influence the price. Contact Tom for a free, no-obligation discussion and quotation for the agreed work.
