Local craftsmanship.
A wider reach.

Based in Colne, we work with homeowners and businesses across Pendle, the Ribble Valley and wider Lancashire, and towards Silsden, Sutton and Cross Hills.

Colne, Pendle & Burnley

Garden rooms, decking, interior joinery and building work close to our Colne base. Tell us what you are planning and share a few photographs so we can discuss a suitable next step.

Clitheroe, Whalley & the Ribble Valley

We cover Clitheroe, Whalley, Barrow and the wider Ribble Valley. Whether the project is an outdoor space, fitted joinery or a larger improvement, we will discuss your brief, access and the work involved.

Silsden, Sutton & Cross Hills

We also take on work in this direction. Include your full postcode when enquiring so we can confirm coverage and discuss a visit for your particular project.

Elsewhere in Lancashire or the North West?

Project size, location and the work required affect what we can take on. Contact Tom with your postcode and a short description of the job; we will confirm whether we can help.

What to include in your enquiry

  • Your postcode and the type of property.
  • Photos of the space and access, with approximate dimensions.
  • The work you want, your priorities and a rough budget.
  • Any drawings or preferred timing you already have.
