How much does a garden room or decking project cost?

The price depends on the size, materials, access, ground preparation and finish. A garden room’s intended use and requirements for insulation, lighting and power also affect the scope. Share your ideas and budget so we can arrange a site visit and prepare a quotation for the agreed work.

Can you build a garden room and decking together?

Yes. Planning both together lets us consider the entrance, steps, seating, levels and the route back to the house. You can also ask about a pergola or other joinery as part of the outdoor space.

Are you insured, and how long have you been established?

Tom Cutts Joinery & Building was established in 2020 and is backed by Tom’s 18+ years of hands-on and managerial experience. We carry up to £5 million public liability insurance for domestic and commercial work.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Colne and cover Pendle, Burnley and wider Lancashire, including Clitheroe, Whalley and Barrow in the Ribble Valley. We also work towards Silsden, Sutton and Cross Hills. Send your postcode and a short description of the work so we can confirm coverage for your project.
