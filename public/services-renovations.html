Home renovations, planned around the way you live.

Improve the home you have with building work and joinery that belong together. Tom Cutts Joinery & Building undertakes renovations and property refurbishments in Colne, Pendle, the Ribble Valley and surrounding areas. We can discuss a single room, a series of improvements or a more extensive change to the property.

Discuss your project ↗

A renovation often involves several connected decisions: what to keep, what to change and which work needs doing first. Tom brings hands-on building experience and project management to that conversation, helping establish an agreed scope before work begins.

Start with the problems you want to solve

A cramped layout, limited storage or tired interiors each call for a different approach. Tell us what works in your home and what frustrates you. We can discuss the priorities, the rooms involved and how building alterations, kitchen fitting or bespoke joinery might fit into a practical programme of improvements.

Understand the building before changing it

Existing conditions affect both the scope and the order of renovation work. Where structural changes or specialist work are proposed, the relevant information needs to be established before that work begins. For older properties, we also discuss the features and materials you want to retain, drawing on our experience of heritage restoration.

Bring the trades and finishing details together

Renovations may involve preparation, building alterations, plumbing, electrical work, plastering and finishing joinery. We can coordinate the trades required for the agreed project and consider how one stage affects the next. Planning doors, skirting, storage and other fitted details early helps carry the intended finish through the whole job.

Keep the scope and communication clear

We discuss what is included, what you are arranging separately and how work will affect access to the property. A clear quotation helps you understand the agreed job. If the scope changes or opening up the building reveals an issue that needs attention, we discuss the implications before agreeing the next step with you.

Useful answers

Before you begin.

Do you take on smaller renovation projects?

Yes, we can discuss individual rooms and specific improvements as well as wider refurbishments. Send a description of the work, photographs and your postcode so we can understand the project.

Can you work on an older property?

Our background includes heritage restoration. Tell us about the building, the work you have in mind and any known restrictions or specialist advice already obtained so we can discuss an appropriate scope.

Can the renovation be completed in stages?

We can discuss phasing where it suits the project. Some tasks depend on earlier work, so the proposed stages need to be considered together before agreeing a practical sequence.

What helps you prepare a renovation quotation?

A room-by-room list of priorities, photographs, your postcode and any drawings are useful. Include your preferred budget and timing, and let us know whether you expect to remain in the property while work takes place.
