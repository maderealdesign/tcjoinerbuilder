Media walls & panelling, made for your room.

Bring your television, storage and display shelves together with a fitted media wall. Tom Cutts Joinery & Building designs the joinery around your room, from the proportions of the screen to the shelves and cupboards you use every day. We work across Colne, Pendle, the Ribble Valley and our wider service area.

Discuss your project ↗

A successful feature wall starts with practical decisions. Tell us what equipment you need to accommodate, what you want to hide away and which details you want on display. Decorative wall panelling can form part of the same brief or be quoted as a separate room improvement.

Plan around the equipment you actually own

Send the television dimensions, its mounting requirements and a list of consoles, speakers or other equipment. We discuss cable routes, ventilation and future access before closing anything into the joinery. If the brief includes a fire or electrical alterations, the appliance specification and appropriate specialist work need to be agreed before construction.

Storage and shelving that suit the room

Open shelves, closed cupboards and display recesses each serve a different purpose. Consider the items you will store, comfortable access and how the unit sits alongside doors, skirting and existing furniture. An elevation sketch and room photographs help us work through the balance before the build.

Wall panelling and the finishing details

From a single feature wall to a hallway or living room, the layout of panelling should work with sockets, corners and openings. We discuss the material, profile, preparation and decoration included in the quotation. Send an example of the look you like and a photograph of the wall rather than relying on a style name alone.

Useful answers

Before you begin.

Can a media wall include a fireplace?

We can discuss a joinery design incorporating a suitable appliance. Share the appliance specification first so clearances, ventilation and any specialist installation requirements can be considered. The quotation will define the work included.

Can you fit panelling without a media wall?

Yes. Wall panelling can be a separate project. Send the wall dimensions, photos showing doors and sockets, and a reference image for the finish you have in mind.

What should I send for a quote?

Your postcode, photos of the whole room, wall measurements and equipment dimensions are a useful start. Include the storage you need and your preferred timing.
