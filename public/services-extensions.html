House extensions that make space for your next chapter.

Create more room where you already feel at home. Tom Cutts Joinery & Building carries out house extensions and garage conversions across Colne, Pendle, the Ribble Valley and surrounding areas. We bring building work and joinery together, with the scope and project coordination discussed from the beginning.

Discuss your project ↗

Your priority might be a larger kitchen, another living area or a better connection to the garden. Tell us what the extra space needs to achieve. Tom’s experience across domestic building and commercial site management helps shape a practical conversation about the work involved.

Turn the idea into a clear building brief

We discuss the proposed use of the space, the existing house and how the extension will connect to it. Drawings, structural information and specifications help define the work and support a detailed quotation. If you are at an earlier stage, we can discuss what information is still needed before the build can be properly priced.

Consider the whole construction process

Groundworks, drainage, the structural shell, roofing and the internal finish are connected parts of an extension. Access and the condition of the existing property also influence the project. We work through the agreed scope so you understand the main stages and where specialist input, inspections and coordination will be required.

Plan the inside alongside the structure

The way a new room works depends on details such as door positions, lighting, kitchen layout and storage. Considering these early helps the structural work and services support the finished space. Our joinery background carries through to doors, skirting, cabinetry and the other details that help the extension feel part of your home.

A programme that recognises you live there

We discuss access, working areas and the points where the project will affect the existing house. Project management can include coordinating the trades involved in the agreed works and keeping you informed of progress. Changes to the scope should be discussed clearly, including their effect on the quotation and programme.

Useful answers

Before you begin.

Can I contact you before my drawings are complete?

Yes. We can discuss your ideas and the information needed to take the project forward. Detailed pricing will depend on the agreed design, specification and relevant structural information.

Do you carry out garage conversions?

Yes. Tell us how you would like to use the space and send photographs of the garage and its connection to the house. We can discuss the work involved and the next steps for your project.

Can you manage the other trades involved?

Yes, project coordination can form part of the agreed service. We discuss the required trades, responsibilities and sequence of work so everyone understands how the build will be managed.

How long will an extension take?

The programme depends on the design, site conditions, materials and the scope of work. We discuss timing for your specific project after reviewing the relevant information, rather than applying one timescale to every extension.
